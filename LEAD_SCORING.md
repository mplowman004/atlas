# Lead qualification and scoring

## Target ICP (marketing filters, not universal credit rules)
- Established Utah operating businesses, ideally 3+ years, annual revenue approximately $1M–$10M, 5–50 employees.
- Prioritize a clear financing event: CRE purchase/refinance or maturity, equipment acquisition, contract-driven expansion, working-capital cycle, business acquisition.
- Loan sizes preferred $500k–$5M, with $100k–$20M exceptions where product fits.
- Personal credit 700+ is a preference only when borrower-provided and appropriate; never infer credit scores from public data.
- Public data rarely verifies cash flow, profitability, documentation readiness or credit. Mark unknown and avoid unjustified high scores.

## Score dimensions (100 points)
1. Clear financing event / use-of-proceeds fit — 20
2. Verified repayment capacity indicators — 20
3. Business maturity — 10
4. Revenue and financing-size fit — 10
5. Event recency and likely timing — 15
6. Verified decision maker access — 10
7. Documentation readiness — 10
8. Relationship/referral potential — 5

## Scoring safeguards
- Every scored field must show evidence or 'unknown'. No points for unsupported assumptions.
- Do not treat a permit or property transfer as proof the company needs financing.
- Scores >=80 will be rare from public data alone; separate a 'research priority' score from borrower-verified 'qualification' if necessary. Do not game scoring to hit daily targets.
- Explain why each product is suggested and show confidence/limitations.
- A+ 85–100; A 70–84; B 50–69; C below 50, all for sales triage only.

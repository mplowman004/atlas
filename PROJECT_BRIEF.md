# Atlas — project brief

## Product
Atlas Lending Intelligence is an existing Replit/GitHub-based commercial lending dashboard and CRM. It has previously worked with Utah County permit/property signals and business enrichment. Existing ingestion and CRM behavior must be inspected, not assumed.

## User and market
Commercial lending professional targeting Utah first, starting with Utah County, then Salt Lake, Davis, Weber, Washington, and other Utah counties. Product priorities: (1) CRE refinance and purchase, owner-occupied and investor where appropriate; (2) equipment; (3) working-capital lines; (4) SBA and acquisitions when eligible. Typical preferred loans $500k–$5M; broader potential $100k–$20M subject to actual lender criteria.

## Core problem
Existing business/permit leads are not sufficiently actionable: missing decision makers, verified contacts, credible reason-to-call, current signals, and follow-up workflows.

## Target outcome
Research up to 25 net-new actionable Utah prospects per day (a quality goal, not a quota to fill with fabricated data). Prefer score >=80/100. Display company, industry, location, business website, business phone, decision maker if verifiable, verified professional email if available, financing signal, source evidence, product fit, outreach angle, score rationale, verification/freshness, and CRM next action.

## Important prior issues
- Stale 2020 permits appeared as recent: correctly parse event dates, source update dates, and ingestion timestamps.
- Multiple permits at the same property should not create duplicate prospects.
- Avoid aggressive or unjustified product classifications.
- Do not remove historical data without backup.
- Utah County coverage must not be limited to Orem.

## Success measures
Contactable lead percentage, signal freshness, sourced lead percentage, duplicate rate, enrichment yield, human acceptance rate, appointments, qualified applications, and eventual funded loans. No fictional funnel conversion guarantees.
